# Content quality rubric

Publish only when all are true:

1. Distinct reader problem and search intent.
2. Direct answer near the beginning.
3. Concrete, original explanation and example.
4. Practical action or exercise.
5. No fabricated research, credentials, outcomes, ratings, or reviews.
6. Accurate, limited book recommendation only when relevant.
7. Unique title, description, canonical URL, and internal links.
8. Manual copyedit, link check, mobile review, and structured-data check.
